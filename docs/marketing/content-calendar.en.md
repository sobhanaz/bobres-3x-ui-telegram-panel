Persian version: [content-calendar.fa.md](content-calendar.fa.md). Posts are in [launch-posts.en.md](launch-posts.en.md), the
messages for operators in [outreach.en.md](outreach.en.md).

Day 1 is the day the release is out and the page is live (see "Before day 1"). The times are a starting guess,
evenings in Tehran time (20:00 to 22:00): watch when your own readers answer and move them.

## Before day 1
- [ ] The release `v0.1.0` is published and the install command works on a clean server (README, "Install").
- [ ] `docs/pricing.md` exists and the tiers table of the page and of post 7 matches it.
- [ ] Pages is on, or you have decided that the repository link is the link (`{{LINK}}` in every text).
- [ ] The channel exists and has a name you own. Nothing here invents one.
- [ ] You have read [claims-checklist.en.md](claims-checklist.en.md) once, and you know where the screenshots'
      "demo data" line is.
- [ ] You can answer a DM within a day for the two weeks. The texts promise help, so keep the promise.

## What to track, by hand
BOBRES has no telemetry and the page has no analytics. Free installs are not counted: only licenses that activate reach your license server, so that is the one number you will see. Count what people tell you:
- questions asked, and which ones came twice;
- DMs sent, answered, and answered with a yes;
- people who say they installed it, and where they got stuck.

Do not write any of these numbers in a public post unless they are exact and the person agreed.

## Rules for every post
- Photos from `docs/marketing/images/` (JPG). Dashboard pictures use demo data; the bot pictures are illustrations:
  say so when you post them.
- No prices, no customer count, no testimonial, no guarantee, no domain or company name that does not exist.
- One call to action per post.
- Reply in the language of the question.

# Operator's own store: terms and privacy notice (template)

> **DRAFT. A lawyer must review this text before it is used.** It is not legal advice and it does not state the law of
> any country. This is a starting point for the **operator**: the person who runs a BOBRES store and sells VPN service to
> their own customers. Replace every `[OPERATOR: ...]` with your own words. `[LAWYER: ...]` is where the right words depend
> on a jurisdiction: ask a lawyer where you and your customers are.

How to use it:

1. Copy the two texts below into your own files and fill in every placeholder. Delete this box.
2. Publish them on a page of your own with an `https://` address.
3. Give the addresses to the bot, so the buttons appear in the bot and on its support screen: in the dashboard, under
   Branding & texts, Store; or in the bot as staff: `/set branding.terms_url https://...` and
   `/set branding.privacy_url https://...`.

These two texts are about **your** store. The Vendor's own terms and privacy policy ([terms-of-service.md](terms-of-service.md),
[privacy-policy.md](privacy-policy.md)) are about BOBRES, your license and what the Vendor receives.

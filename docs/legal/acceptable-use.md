## 3. What the Operator must not do to BOBRES or the Vendor

- Pass a license key or token to anyone else, sell it, or use one that the Vendor did not issue for the Operator's
  install.
- Disable, bypass or tamper with the license checks or the free tier limits, or remove the "Powered by BOBRES" mark
  without a license that includes white label (the [LICENSE](../../LICENSE), section 3).
- Host or run BOBRES for others, resell it, or offer it as a service of the Operator's own without a license that allows it.
- Attack, overload or probe the license server, or work around its rate limits.
- Use the BOBRES name, the beaver or the brand assets in a way that suggests the Vendor backs the Operator's store.

## 1. How a license is bought

A license is a **subscription per install**, monthly or yearly. The Operator pays through a channel the Vendor offers
([OWNER: channels]) and receives a license key. The Vendor does not charge automatically: **to renew, the Operator pays
again.** The tiers and the prices shown when the Operator orders are in [pricing.md](../pricing.md).

## 2. Cancelling

- To cancel, **stop paying**. There is nothing else to do and no cancellation fee. The Operator may also tell the Vendor
  ([OWNER: contact]), so that the license is not renewed by mistake.
- The license stays active until the end of the paid period.
- After that comes a grace period (14 days by default): everything still works, with a warning in the dashboard.
- When the grace period ends, the install **falls to the free tier**: 1 server, 25 active services, 2 staff seats,
  no resellers, no white label ("Powered by BOBRES" is shown again), no automatic USDT. **Nothing is deleted**: the data,
  the customers' services, the servers and the staff accounts stay, and existing services keep working, renewing and
  topping up. Only new things beyond the free tier's limits are refused until the license is renewed: customers are
  told that new purchases are temporarily unavailable once 25 services are active, and the staff get an alert.
- Renewing later brings the paid features back. Nothing needs to be reinstalled.

## 4. When a license is revoked

If the Vendor revokes a license for a breach of the [Terms of Service](terms-of-service.md) or the
[Acceptable Use Policy](acceptable-use.md), or because a payment was reversed (a chargeback), the rest of the paid period
is not refunded. [LAWYER: whether that is allowed, and the notice owed.] The install goes back to the free tier and nothing

# Privacy Policy

This is how **{store}** handles your data when you use our Telegram bot, its Mini App and our website. The store runs on software that we operate on our own server: nobody else receives your data from it.

## What we collect

- **Your Telegram details**: your Telegram ID, username and language, and when you joined. If you came through an invite link, who invited you.
- **What you buy**: your orders, the plans and services you have, their dates, the traffic used, and your wallet with the list of its movements.
- **Your payments**: the amount, the method and its reference, and the receipt image or transaction id you send us as proof.
- **Your messages to support**: the text, photos and files you send.
- **Technical records**: when our staff act on your account, and the addresses they act from.

We do not ask for your name, phone number or e-mail address. We do not see what you do through the VPN: we only read how much traffic a service has used.

## Why

To sell you the service, deliver it and renew it; to take payments and keep our accounts; to answer you; to stop abuse and fraud; and to send you reminders and notices about your service.

## Who sees it

- Our staff, as far as their work needs it.
- Telegram, because you talk to us through it.
- The payment provider you pick, for your payment.
- Our server host, and the VPN panel that holds your service.

We do not sell your data.

## How long

We keep your account, orders and money records for as long as you use the store and as long as we need them for our accounts. Records of money movements cannot be erased one by one: if we delete your account, we remove who you are from them and keep the amounts. Backups of the store are encrypted.

## Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data, or to stop sending you messages. Write to {contact}.

## Security

The store's secrets are encrypted, our staff sign in with a password and an authenticator code, and our dashboard uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly safe: if we learn that your data was exposed, we will tell you.

## Changes

We may change this policy. The current version is always on this page.

Version: draft 0.1, [OWNER: date]. It covers the paid licenses (Standard and Pro) that the Vendor, [OWNER: legal name], sells to operators. It does not cover what an operator sells to their own customers.

1. How a license is bought

A license is a subscription per install, monthly or yearly. The Operator pays through a channel the Vendor offers ([OWNER: channels]) and receives a license key. The Vendor does not charge automatically: to renew, the Operator pays again. The tiers and the prices shown when the Operator orders are in pricing.md.

2. Cancelling

  • To cancel, stop paying. There is nothing else to do and no cancellation fee. The Operator may also tell the Vendor ([OWNER: contact]), so that the license is not renewed by mistake.
  • The license stays active until the end of the paid period.
  • After that comes a grace period (14 days by default): everything still works, with a warning in the dashboard.
  • When the grace period ends, the install falls to the free tier: 1 server, 25 active services, 2 staff seats, no resellers, no white label ("Powered by BOBRES" is shown again), no automatic USDT. Nothing is deleted: the data, the customers' services, the servers and the staff accounts stay, and existing services keep working, renewing and topping up. Only new things beyond the free tier's limits are refused until the license is renewed: customers are told that new purchases are temporarily unavailable once 25 services are active, and the staff get an alert.
  • Renewing later brings the paid features back. Nothing needs to be reinstalled.

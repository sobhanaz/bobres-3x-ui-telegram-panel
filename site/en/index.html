What it does

From a customer's first message to their renewal

Three parts that work together, and all three are yours.

For customers: a Telegram bot

Persian and English, with a bottom menu and colored buttons. A customer picks a plan, pays, and gets the subscription link and a QR code in the same chat. Renewals, extra traffic, a wallet, a free trial, invites and support live in the same bot.

For you: a web dashboard

A dashboard with roles

Your brand

Removing the BOBRES mark: Pro

Backups

Every day, encrypted: the newest 7 on the server, each one (up to 50 MB) sent to the owner on Telegram, and optionally a copy in S3 storage. Restoring takes one command.

System alerts on Telegram

When a service, a 3x-ui server, a gateway, the disk, a backup or an HTTPS certificate has a problem, you hear about it on Telegram, and again when it is fixed.

HTTPS manager

An automatic certificate for your domain (the default), a Let's Encrypt certificate for the server's IP address, or your own certificate and key. From the dashboard or with bobres ssl.

Install, update and roll back

How it works

Three steps to your first sale

  1. Get ready

    You need four things:

    • An Ubuntu or Debian server
    • A domain that points to it
    • A bot from @BotFather and your numeric Telegram ID
    • A 3x-ui panel with an API token

  2. Run one command

    The installer verifies the release's signature, asks for your domain, owner ID, bot token and panel (tokens are typed hidden), generates every secret and starts the store.

  3. Add plans and sell

    Send /dashboard to your bot and open the one-time login link. Set up your plans and payment methods, and your store is ready for customers.

FAQ

Plain answers

What do I need to install it?
  • A server with Ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04, or Debian 11, 12 or 13 (amd64 or arm64), 1 GB of RAM (2 GB is better), 10 GB of free disk, ports 80 and 443 free, and root access.
  • A domain whose A record points to the server.
  • A new Telegram bot from @BotFather, and your numeric Telegram ID.
  • A 3x-ui panel with an API token. Versions 3.8.5 to 3.9.0 are tested, and you can add panels later in the dashboard.

The installer installs Docker if it is missing.

Do I need 3x-ui? Does it work with older versions?

Yes. BOBRES creates and manages users on 3x-ui panels. For now only version 3 is supported, tested on 3.8.5 and 3.9.0. There is no adapter for older versions yet; we build one only when a customer needs it.

Which payment methods are there?
  • The wallet, always on.
  • Card to card, USDT (TRC20 or ERC20) and a Zarinpal payment link (optional): the customer sends a receipt photo (for USDT, a transaction ID is enough) and you approve it from the review queue. A repeated reference number is flagged.
  • Telegram Stars, at your own rate in Toman.
  • Zarinpal online (optional), off until you enter a merchant ID.
  • Automatic USDT on TRON (TRC20): each payment gets its own exact amount and 45 minutes to pay, and an exact match settles by itself (Standard and up).
What does online Zarinpal need?

Zarinpal online needs your own approved merchant account. We have not verified that Zarinpal accepts VPN stores, so ask Zarinpal's support before you switch it on. Card to card, USDT and Telegram Stars work without it.

Who owns the data and the customers?

You do. BOBRES runs on your server, with your domain, bot and panels. Your customers, orders and wallets stay in that server's database, and backups are encrypted with a backup key.

Nothing about your customers is sent to us. Only an install that uses online license activation talks to the project's license server (which, like any server, sees the address a request comes from). At activation it sends the key, the install ID, the domain and the version. The daily refresh sends the installed license token and the version. Free installs that never activate send nothing. Releases are downloaded from GitHub. (Your bot, of course, talks to Telegram, your payment providers and your panels, as it must.)

One more request: in the HTTPS server-IP mode, the bot asks api.ipify.org (then ifconfig.me) for the server's public address. No customer data is in that request.

How do updates work?

With sudo bobres update. It verifies the release's signature, makes a backup, keeps a restore point and rolls back by itself if the new version does not come up healthy. If you change your mind later, sudo bobres rollback undoes the last update. There are stable and beta channels.

Licensing, in plain words

The core is free: the bot, the dashboard, the manual payment methods, Stars, Zarinpal (optional), backups and HTTPS. The Free tier covers 1 server, up to 25 active services and 2 staff seats.

If you need resellers, automatic USDT, more servers and services, or no BOBRES mark, you take a subscription for each install: Standard or Pro. Every new install starts with a 30-day trial of the premium features (everything except removing the BOBRES mark). Prices: Standard is 4 USDT a month and Pro is 12 USDT a month, paid in USDT (TRC20); a year is billed as ten months.

What happens when a subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your customers and their services keep working, and your install goes back to the Free tier. A license never stops what already exists; it only affects new actions.

What security measures does it have?
  • Four separate services, each with its own database role and token; the 3x-ui tokens live only in the provisioner and the gateway secrets only in payments.
  • Secrets are encrypted at rest, with a separate key for each service.
  • Releases are signed with Ed25519, and BOBRES's own Docker images with cosign. The Postgres, Redis and Caddy images come from others and are pinned by version (Caddy also by digest).
  • The dashboard has CSRF tokens, authenticator codes for every password login, a strict CSP and an append-only audit log.
  • BOBRES's own services run as an unprivileged user, with a read-only filesystem and no extra capabilities.

These are measures, not a guarantee. Review the install yourself before you open it to the public.

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. There is a free tier with the limits above, and every new install starts with a 30-day trial of the premium features, everything except removing the BOBRES mark.
